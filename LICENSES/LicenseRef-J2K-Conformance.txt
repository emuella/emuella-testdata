Licence reference: JPEG 2000 conformance files

The authoritative current insert is identified by the JPEG 2000 conformance
manifest. Its embedded COPYRIGHT.txt carries a JPEG-2000-use-only copyright
non-assertion for conformance-related use and requires preservation of the
notice in copies or derivative works. The ISO portal separately limits its
download to original-format use for purposes specified by the standard.

The locked pack follows the stricter original-format condition. Neither the
embedded notice nor public downloadability is treated as an Apache-2.0 grant,
and redistribution is not inferred. The complete upstream notices remain in
the externally materialized tree and are verified by digest.
